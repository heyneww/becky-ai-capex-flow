校验依据：谁重算、谁漏算Basis of verification: who double-counts, who misses

  • 一手文件优先。我们的债务 134 笔里 112 笔挂 SEC 文件（424B2 / 424B5 定价说明书、8-K 第 1.01 / 2.03 项、6-K），其余靠公司新闻稿或两家媒体正文。helajo 的来源以媒体报道为主，少数挂公司公告。两边出现金额差时，以定价说明书的本金为准。
  • 按发行逐笔记，不按新闻合并。EDGAR 每一次定价都是一笔，所以 Alphabet 2025 年 4 月的欧元档与美元档、CoreWeave 2026 年 4 月的 9.75% 票据和两天后的增发，在我们这边各是一条；helajo 按新闻口径常合成一条或漏掉。这是「我们独有」里 23 笔的主要来源。
  • 整笔记 SPV 融资，不拆层。Anthropic 博通芯片 SPV 的 630 亿我们记一笔；helajo 拆成 435 亿第一留置权加未披露的第二留置权。SpaceX 的 400 亿 GPU 融资我们按 8-K「主要为可赎回债务」记债务；helajo 记为租赁 400 亿，另把 PIMCO 认购的 40 亿 A2 票据记进债务。看总量两边都有，看分项会错位。
  • 已偿还、被置换的不计。Core Scientific 两笔 5 亿过桥、xAI 2025 年 50 亿债务、软银两笔过桥（150 亿、200 亿）helajo 保留在清单里，我们只计存续的那笔。
  • 范围：只收与 AI 算力、数据中心、芯片产能挂钩的融资。苹果、阿里、百度、腾讯、博通、AMD、美光、SK 海力士的常规公司债 helajo 计入（20 笔 704 亿），我们不收。反过来，Equinix、Digital Realty、Marvell、超微、Bloom 的发债我们收（20 笔 299 亿），他们不收。这是范围定义的差异，不是谁错。
  • 候选不计。单一媒体来源或置信不足的留在候选区（Zankore 31 亿、Lambda 摩根大通 10 亿），有一手文件或第二家媒体后自动升级。
  • 本版仍缺的 8 笔。Nexus Data Centers 两笔 169 亿已于 10-29 收编补入（Evercore 8-K 附件给出 156 亿）；剩下的 Alphabet 日元债、亚马逊瑞郎债不走 EDGAR，其余见逐笔对照的备注。这些是我们漏算，不是口径差。
  • Primary filings first. 112 of our 134 debt edges cite SEC filings (424B2 / 424B5 pricing supplements, 8-K items 1.01 / 2.03, 6-K); the rest rest on company press releases or two independent media reports with full text. helajo relies mostly on press coverage, with some company announcements. Where amounts differ, the principal in the pricing supplement wins.
  • One edge per issuance, not per news story. Every EDGAR pricing is its own edge, so Alphabet's April 2025 euro and dollar tranches, or CoreWeave's April 2026 9.75% notes and the tap two days later, are separate rows for us; helajo often folds them into one story or misses them. This is where most of the 23 "ours only, extra" rows come from.
  • SPV financings are booked whole, not by tranche. We book the $63B Anthropic–Broadcom chip SPV loan as one edge; helajo lists the $43.5B first lien plus an undisclosed second lien. SpaceX's $40B GPU financing is debt for us per the 8-K ("primarily redeemable debt"); helajo books it as a $40B lease and separately counts PIMCO's $4B A2 notes as debt. Totals are present on both sides, but the buckets differ.
  • Repaid or refinanced loans are excluded. Core Scientific's two $500M bridges, xAI's 2025 $5B debt and SoftBank's two bridge loans ($15B, $20B) stay in helajo's list; we count only the surviving facility.
  • Scope: only financing tied to AI compute, data centers or chip capacity. helajo counts routine corporate bonds from Apple, Alibaba, Baidu, Tencent, Broadcom, AMD, Micron and SK hynix (20 rows, $70.4B); we do not. Conversely we count Equinix, Digital Realty, Marvell, Supermicro and Bloom issuance (20 rows, $29.9B) that they leave out. This is a scope definition difference, not an error on either side.
  • Candidates do not count. Single-source or low-confidence items stay in the candidate zone (Zankore $3.1B, Lambda's JPMorgan $1B) until a filing or a second outlet arrives, then upgrade automatically.
  • Eight genuine gaps remain in this version. Nexus Data Centers' two financings ($16.9B) were adopted and added on Oct 29 (the $15.6B figure comes from Evercore's 8-K exhibit); Alphabet's yen bond and Amazon's Swiss franc bond are not on EDGAR; the rest are annotated row by row. These are omissions on our side, not scope differences.
